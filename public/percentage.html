Percentages

Learn the important formulas before starting your practice.

1. Basic Percentage

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100
Example: What percentage is 20 of 100?

(20 ÷ 100) × 100 = 20%

2. Percentage of a Number

X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y
Example: Find 25% of 200.

(25 ÷ 100) × 200 = 50

3. Percentage Increase

Percentage Increase = (Increase ÷ Original Value) × 100
Example: A price increases from ₹100 to ₹120.

Increase = 20
Percentage Increase = (20 ÷ 100) × 100 = 20%

4. Percentage Decrease

Percentage Decrease = (Decrease ÷ Original Value) × 100
Example: A price decreases from ₹200 to ₹150.

Decrease = 50
Percentage Decrease = (50 ÷ 200) × 100 = 25%

5. Successive Percentage Change

Net Change = a + b + (ab ÷ 100)

Use positive values for increases and negative values for decreases.

Example: A number increases by 20% and then by 10%.

Net change = 20 + 10 + (20 × 10 ÷ 100)

= 30 + 2 = 32%
