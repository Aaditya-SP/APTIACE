Thoughts & Principles
“

It doesn't matter how it's done. It doesn't matter what needs to be sacrificed. In this world, winning is everything.

“

Your shortcoming is that you assume others will hold you back, and put them at a distance from the start.

“

Relying on others won't get you to the top.

“

Having power and not knowing how to use it is foolish.

“

There is only one law in love: it is to make your loved one happy.

「愛にはたったひとつの決まりしかない。それは愛する者を幸福に導くことだ」
Il n'y a qu'une loi en sentiment. C'est de faire le bonheur de ce qu'on aime.
“

Change your desires rather than the order of the world.

「欲望のために世界を変えるのではなく、まず己を変えよ」
À changer mes désirs que l'ordre du monde.
